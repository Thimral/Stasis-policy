Privacy Policy for Stasis

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health data, or device information.

2. Local storage only

Everything you enter — sleep logs, preferences, settings — is stored exclusively on your own device, in a local SQLite database (via the Drift database library), inside the app's private, sandboxed storage. We have no servers, no databases, and no access to your information.

3. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, web beacons, fingerprinting, or any tracking technologies. The app makes no network requests except the one described below. The only persistent data on your device is your sleep logs and settings.

4. Third-party services

We do not integrate with any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. The only external service is Google Play, which processes your one-time Stasis Pro purchase through the Google Play Billing Library. Buying Pro, restoring it and checking that you have bought it go through it. We do not receive or store your payment details: those are handled entirely by Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that part. The app has no internet permission of its own.

5. Sleep mode and permissions

Sleep mode uses Android's Do Not Disturb access, which you grant in system settings, to silence notifications while you sleep. Stasis only switches the setting on and off at the times you choose and restores what you had; it does not read your notifications, calls or contacts. Notifications are used for your reminders. The alarm-clock shortcut hands a time to your clock app and nothing else.

6. Things you start that leave the app, the widget and recent apps

The app never sends anything by itself. These hand over to another app only when you choose: exporting a CSV or a sleep summary for your doctor (to the sharing app you pick), setting a wake-up alarm (your clock app), and rating the app (four or five stars open the Google Play page so you can leave a public review; a lower rating with a comment opens an email draft to the developer, and the comment is only sent if you send that email, so please do not put health details in it). The optional home screen widget counts down the time to the bedtime you set; it is worked out on your phone from your schedule and nothing is sent anywhere. "Hide Stasis in recent apps" keeps the app out of the recent-apps list and screenshots. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so your data is not copied to the cloud or to a new phone.

7. Children's privacy

Stasis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age — consistent with Section 1, we collect no data at all.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / POPIA)

Because we hold zero data about you, there is nothing to access, rectify, or delete. All your data is under your physical control on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" inside Settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy — for example, adding an optional cloud feature — we will flag it clearly in the app. This version remains valid for the fully offline version of the app.
